# Stage 1: Scientific Laboratory Design (Asymmetric Co-op)

## Overview & Concept
Stage 1 is an asymmetric cooperative puzzle experience. The two players are separated in environments with identical room structures. Communication is restricted exclusively to voice (e.g., via walkie-talkies). The core theme is a world/laboratory that is collapsing, requiring the players to cooperate to restore power and stabilize a dimensional machine.

**Design Considerations (John's Comments):**
- *World Collapse:* Enhance the feeling that the lab and the world are collapsing.
- *Player A Enhancements:* Consider giving Player A a mechanical aspect, such as special glasses or an ability to see broken machine parts, to increase immersion.
- *Player B Enhancements:* Consider giving Player B specific fire-related abilities or interactions.

## 1. Intro Sequence
1. **Initial State:** Both players begin in a normal, well-lit room.
2. **The Incident:** The NPC Researcher says "Press the start button for device recovery!". When pressed, the wall breaks/dimension shifts, revealing the other user's room.
3. **Recovery Attempt:** The machine is restarted to try and fix the wall.
4. **Blackout:** The machine runs out of battery, causing Player A's room to completely black out. Connection with the NPC Researcher is lost.

## 2. Asymmetric Puzzle Progression

### Puzzle 1: Illumination
- **Player A:** Room is completely dark. A stationary fire sphere in the corner provides minimal light but cannot be moved.
- **Player B:** Finds a fire sphere wrapped in glass. B must move this fire sphere to illuminate A's room.

### Puzzle 2: Battery Thawing
- **Player A:** Finds a battery that is frozen in solid ice. Player A places it into the thawing device.
- **Player B:** Places their fire sphere into the thawing device on their side to provide the necessary heat.
- **Resolution:** The battery thaws. Player A inserts the thawed battery, and electricity returns to A's room.

### Puzzle 3: Pipe Repair & Pressure
- **The Complication:** Restoring power causes the machine to restart, but it overheats and a pipe bursts.
- **Player B:** Must physically repair the broken pipe.
- **Player A:** Must turn a pipe valve to lower the pressure inside the system.

### Puzzle 4: The Password
- **Player B:** Discovers the Researcher's Diary. The diary reveals a hint for the password: `Date + Number of filled liquid bottles (out of 10)`. The diary notes that you must "shake" the bottles to check.
- **Player A:** Has the 10 bottles in their room. Player A shakes them to determine how many are full.
- **Resolution:** Player A combines the date and the bottle count to input the correct password into the machine.

## 3. Ending Sequence
- **Stabilization:** Password input stabilizes the dimensional device.
- **Transition:** The video call with the NPC Researcher reconnects. The Researcher says, "Oh, you fixed it! Wait, no? BOOM!"
- **Next Stage:** The explosion transitions the players into Stage 2.
